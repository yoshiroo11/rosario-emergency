Welcome

This project shows how a municipality can receive emergency reports from residents, locate them on a map, and give its response team a live queue of what needs attention first.

🚨 Send an Emergency Report

About this project

In any growing town, emergencies can happen in many places at once. Residents may not know who to call, and responders may not know exactly where to send help. This concept lets anyone report an emergency with their location, and lets the municipality's response team see every report in order and mark it as Waiting, On-going, or Done.

📍

Location included

Reports can carry GPS coordinates so responders know where to go.
📋

Live queue

Responders see reports in order, with Waiting, On-going and Done lists.
🔒

Private dashboard

Only authorized response staff can log in and view the reports.

Emergency types handled

🚑

Medical

Injury, sudden illness, or a person who needs urgent medical help.
🔥

Fire

Fire or smoke in a house, building, or open area.
🚗

Road accident

Vehicle collisions and road incidents that need help or traffic control.
🚓

Crime / security

Theft, violence, or any threat to public safety.
🌊

Flood / disaster

Flooding, landslides, and other disaster situations.
